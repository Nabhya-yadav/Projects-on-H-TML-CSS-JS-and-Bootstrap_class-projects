Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a giant white marble building in Agra, India. Emperor Shah Jahan built it for his wife Mumtaz Mahal. It took 22 years to build, starting in 1632 and finishing in 1653. Today, it is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a famous wonder of the world.

Ajantha Caves

The Ajanta Caves are a collection of roughly 30 rock-cut Buddhist cave monuments in Maharashtra, India, carved into a horseshoe-shaped cliff. Dating from the 2nd century BCE to the 6th century CE, this UNESCO World Heritage Site is famous for its ancient monasteries, prayer halls, and masterwork mural paintings.

Golden Temple

The Golden Temple, formally known as Sri Harmandir Sahib or Darbar Sahib, is the holiest shrine in Sikhism. Located in Amritsar, Punjab, India, it is renowned for its stunning gold-plated upper dome, peaceful holy pool (Amrit Sarovar), and profound message of universal equality and openness to all people regardless of faith
